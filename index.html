CarMeetz Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the CarMeetz mobile app ("CarMeetz", "we", "us") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. CarMeetz is operated by GoNextApps. If you have questions, contact us at appsgonext@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password, used to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Display name and, if you choose to add one, a home region.

Content you add

  • Your garage: vehicles (make, model, year, trim, nickname, colour, description), modifications, and photos you choose from your photo library.
  • Clubs: clubs you create or join, including club names, descriptions, logos and your role in them.
  • Meets and cruises: meets you post (title, description, rules, time, and the location you pick), meets you RSVP to, and the vehicle you bring.
  • Routes: routes you save, including their start point and stops.

Location

If you allow it, CarMeetz uses your device's location while the app is in use to show meets near you and to help you place meets and routes on the map. Your current location is used on your device and is not stored on our servers. We only store locations you deliberately choose to save, such as the place of a meet you post or the stops on a route. CarMeetz does not track your location in the background. You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings and still use the app.

Photos

CarMeetz asks for access to your photo library only so you can add photos of your vehicles and club logos. We upload only the photos you pick. The app does not use your camera or microphone.

Purchases

If you buy an upgrade, the payment is handled by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive and store a record of the purchase (the product, the store, a transaction ID and the date) so we can give you what you bought.

Technical information

Like most online services, our providers automatically process basic technical information when the app connects to them, such as IP address, device type, operating system and app version, for security and to keep the service running. CarMeetz does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your information.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your garage, clubs, meets and routes to you and to other members, as described below.
  • To show meets and routes near you and to calculate road routes.
  • To process purchases and restore them on your devices.
  • To send emails about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To keep CarMeetz secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

What other users can see

CarMeetz is a community app. Other signed-in users can see:

  • Your display name and home region.
  • Your garage, including your vehicles, photos and modifications.
  • Clubs you belong to, meets you post, and meets you have said you are going to.
  • Meets marked public are visible to all signed-in users, while members-only meets are visible only to members of that club.

Your email address is never shown to other users. Saved routes are private to you unless you attach them to a meet.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run CarMeetz, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and sign-in system, and sends account emails.
  • RevenueCat: manages in-app purchases. It receives your CarMeetz user ID and purchase details.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: process payments for purchases.
  • Google Maps: displays maps in the app and opens turn-by-turn directions when you ask for them. Your device's built-in geocoding service may be used to turn a map point into a street address.
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine): calculates road routes. Only the route's map points are sent, with nothing that identifies you.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described below. Copies may remain in backups for a short period before they are overwritten. Purchase records held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open Profile, tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes:

  • Your profile and sign-in details.
  • Your garage: vehicles, photos and modifications.
  • Your club memberships and join requests.
  • Your RSVPs to meets and cruises.
  • Your saved routes.
  • Any unused club credits. Purchases are not refunded.

Clubs you own are handed over to another member so the community is not lost. A club with no other members is deleted along with its meets. Meets you posted in clubs that continue may remain, but they are no longer linked to you.

If you can't access the app, email appsgonext@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will do so within 30 days.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete individual pieces of your CarMeetz data at any time and keep your account:

  • Vehicles, photos and modifications: open Garage, choose a vehicle, and delete the vehicle, a photo or a modification.
  • Saved routes: open the route and delete it.
  • Meets you posted: open the meet and cancel or delete it.
  • Clubs: leave a club, or delete a club you own.
  • RSVPs: open the meet and remove your RSVP.
  • Profile details: change your display name, or edit or clear your home region, in Profile.

Deleted items are removed from our servers straight away. Copies may remain in backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

To ask us to delete any other data, email appsgonext@gmail.com from the address on your account and tell us what you want deleted. We will do so within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted so that only you can change your own content. Photos are stored privately and shown to signed-in users through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

CarMeetz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to how it is used. You can do most of this directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at appsgonext@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CarMeetz changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
